2025–2026 Audience Research Strategy Measurement

Market Research for Word-of-Mouth Strategy

A multi-volume market research and marketing strategy project for a regional multi-location service business, examining how an existing relationship-led audience consumes media and how those patterns could inform word-of-mouth growth, content, channels, search, and measurement.

Research was treated as the beginning of the decision process, not the end. Subsequent performance was used to test and refine the original strategic recommendations.

Audience Research Statistical Analysis Content Strategy Channel Strategy Search Strategy Lifecycle Marketing Measurement

Research · Analysis · Strategy · Measurement

Project Overview

From audience research to a testable marketing strategy.

The project studied how a relationship-led audience uses social platforms, streaming, websites, entertainment, sports, and other media, then translated those patterns into practical decisions for content, channels, search, and measurement.

163 valid responses
50 reported ZIP codes
2025 research period
2026 performance lookback
01

Study the audience without overstating the sample.

The survey was recruited through existing relationship networks, so findings were used to understand this audience rather than claim statewide representation.

02

Look for patterns that could change a decision.

Platform overlap, content affinities, local media use, and other relationships were analyzed to distinguish useful strategic signals from simple popularity.

03

Let performance challenge the research.

The recommendations were later compared with actual marketing performance so assumptions could be retained, revised, or tested again.
